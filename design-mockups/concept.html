The direction we're building toward.

Soft-monster cards for the public board, three-pane admin with a detail-dominant layout, dedicated review queue, public-approval gate before anything goes live, optional voice notes with Gemini transcription, and a single page showing all five source links.

✓soft monster cards ✓three-pane admin · detail dominant ✓review queue + "caught up" ✓public approval gate ✓5-source attribution ✓admin lens on public ✓addressed feed v3voice notes
Phasing: v1 = core text-only suggestion box (no AI, no advanced integrations). v2 = Discord bot + integrations. v3 = AI (Gemini) + voice notes (designed but deferred until transcription is available).
01 · admin

Three-pane, detail-dominant

Sidebar narrow (filters/segments), list narrower than before (just enough to scan titles), detail panel takes the rest. The thin handles between panes are drag-to-resize. Public-approval toggle lives at the top of the detail so it's never a hidden action.

03 · public approval gate

Nothing goes public without you

Submitters can request their suggestion be public. It doesn't auto-show. Until you flip the toggle in the detail view, it lives in the admin only. Eligible only when status is on the board / planned / building — never new (you haven't reviewed) and never shipped/passed (it's done).

03b · admin lens

View public-as-visitor, or admin-on-public

Same URL. Same content. Two lenses. When you're logged in as admin and visit the public board, a toggle at the top lets you flip between exactly-what-visitors-see and admin-on-public — same layout, plus inline actions on every card (resolve, remove from public, jump to admin detail). No separate "admin clone" page.

04a · submitter · v3 only

Voice note recording flow v3 — designed, deferred

The voice feature is fully deferred to v3 — it depends on Gemini transcription, so it ships when AI ships. v1 and v2 submission forms are text-only. UX below is the v3 target so we don't have to redesign later.

step 1 · permission

Ask politely

First-time recorders get a friendly explanation before the browser permission dialog. No surprises.

microphone access We'd love to hear it. Browser will ask in a sec — your audio stays on your device until you hit submit.
step 2 · recording

Tap to record

Live waveform, timer counts up. 60-second cap shown subtly. Tap again to stop.

Listen, retry, or attach

Plays back. Don't like it? Re-record — costs nothing (it's all client-side). When you hit submit, we transcribe on our end.
